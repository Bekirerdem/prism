# Try it in 5 minutes (testnet)

*Türkçe versiyon: [Hızlı başlangıç](/try-it-tr)*

Everything below runs on **testnet**: no real money, zero risk. Every action is signed
by you — non-custodial, funds stay under your control the whole time.

**App:** [eunomia.finance](https://eunomia.finance)

## Two ways in

**With a passkey (fastest).** Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN. No wallet to
install, no seed phrase to write down, and no XLM to find first — the passkey controls a
Stellar smart wallet and transaction fees are sponsored. Works the same on a phone.

**With a wallet.** If you already have one: Freighter, xBull, Albedo, LOBSTR, Rabet or
Hana on desktop; any WalletConnect-capable Stellar wallet on a phone. Switch it to
**Testnet** in the wallet's own settings first.

## Steps

1. **Sign in** — *Create your treasury with a passkey*, and confirm with your face,
   fingerprint or PIN. (Prefer a wallet? *I have a wallet* → pick yours. On a phone,
   choose **WalletConnect** and scan the QR.)
2. **Set your rules and create** — a daily cap and a per-payment cap → *Create treasury*
   → confirm. When it finishes, **hit "Copy ID" and save your treasury ID**, or use
   *Back up on Stellar* in Settings so it can be found from any device with the same
   passkey.
3. **Fund it** — enter an amount (e.g. 20) → *Fund* → confirm. A wallet user with an
   empty balance gets a one-click **"Get test XLM"** button first.
4. **Approve a payee** — on **Payments**, add an address that may be paid. No second
   address handy? Click **"use the sample vendor"**.
5. **Send a payment** — pay your approved address within the caps → it lands on-chain ✓.
6. **Now watch the real show** — try an amount **over** your cap, or a payment to an
   address you never approved: it is **rejected on-chain** and funds never move.
   That rejection is the product working. 🔴
7. **Hand it to an agent (no more popups)** — in the **Agent** tab set a spending cap
   and a duration → *Start Leash* (one approval). Payments now sign with the session
   key — try **Run autonomous task**: 1 XLM lands on-chain with **zero popups**,
   every rule still applies. *Revoke Leash* takes control back instantly.
8. **Owner controls** — in **Settings**: *Pause spending* (freezes the agent, withdraw
   still works), *Withdraw* funds back out, *Update limits* live. The owner always has
   an exit.

## If something goes wrong

Errors are shown in plain language inside the app. The **Share feedback** button
(bottom right) opens a short form — two sentences there directly shape the roadmap. 🙏
